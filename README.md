# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI Sales Dashboard** developed to analyze the performance of an online store and provide a clear view of **sales, orders, customers, products, and product categories**.

The dashboard transforms transactional sales data into meaningful business insights that can help stakeholders monitor performance, identify top-performing products, and understand sales trends.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to build an interactive sales analytics dashboard that answers key business questions:

* How are sales performing over time?
* How many orders and customers does the store have?
* Which products generate the highest sales?
* Which product categories contribute most to revenue?
* How does sales performance vary across countries?
* How does performance change over different time periods?

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard contains several key analytical components.

### 🔢 KPI Cards

The top section provides a quick overview of the store's performance:

* **Total Orders:** 100
* **Total Customers:** 12
* **Total Sales:** ~1M

These KPIs provide stakeholders with an immediate understanding of overall business performance.

### 📈 Sales Over Time

A line chart visualizes sales trends across the selected period.

This helps identify:

* Sales growth and decline
* Peak sales periods
* Changes in sales performance
* Potential seasonal patterns

Users can interact with the **date range slicer** to analyze specific periods.


### 🏆 Sales by Products

A horizontal bar chart ranks products according to their sales contribution.

Some of the products included in the analysis are:

* ThinkPad X1
* Dell XPS 15
* iPhone 16
* Galaxy S24
* HP Spectre x360
* iPad Air 6
* Echo Dot 6
* Google Nest Hub
* Surface Go 4
* Galaxy Tab S10
* MacBook Air M4

This visualization helps identify the **top revenue-generating products**.

### 📦 Sales by Categories

The dashboard analyzes sales contribution across different product categories:

* 💻 Laptop
* 📱 Smartphone
* 📲 Tablet
* 🏠 Smart Home
* 🔌 Accessory

The category-level analysis helps identify which product groups contribute most to overall sales.

## 🌍 Interactive Filters

The dashboard allows users to dynamically explore the data using:

### Date

Analyze sales within a selected date range.

### Country

Filter performance by:

* China
* Germany
* India
* United States

### Product Category

Analyze individual categories such as Laptop, Smartphone, Tablet, Smart Home, and Accessory.

These filters make the dashboard interactive and allow users to perform more focused analysis.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard can help an online store answer questions such as:

**Product Performance**

> Which products are generating the most revenue?

**Category Performance**

> Which product category is the strongest contributor to sales?

**Sales Trends**

> Are sales increasing or declining over time?

**Geographic Performance**

> Which countries are generating the highest sales?

> How many orders and customers are contributing to overall revenue?
